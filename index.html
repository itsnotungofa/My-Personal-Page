BREAKING NEWS

Scientifics discover that the cure for hapiness is building a domain

Published by Ungofa in Oct 26, 2026

In a finding that has left the scientific community staring at its own screens, researchers announced today that the long-sought cure for hapiness is not a pill, a vaccine or a very good nap, but building a domain.

The study followed one subject through the full procedure: registering a name, pointing it at the right nameservers, adding four A records and one CNAME, and waiting for the DNS check to turn green. According to the team, the subject reported a sharp rise in hapiness at the exact moment the page loaded on its own address.

"We expected a placebo effect," said the lead researcher, adjusting his goggles. "Instead, we saw a 404 turn into a homepage, and that was when the hapiness peaked."

Side effects are reported to be mild. They include checking the site on three different devices, refreshing with Ctrl+F5 more than medically advisable, and sending the link to friends who did not ask for it.

The team stresses that the treatment works best when the domain is free, the page is yours and the first visitor is you. A follow-up study is already planned, though the researchers admit they are not sure whether a second domain would cure anything or just start a collection.
